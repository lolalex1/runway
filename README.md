# Runway

A simple, modern take on **true expense planning**.

Runway helps you forecast your money — not track it.
No bank connections. No logins. No scraped data.
Just honest, manual inputs and clear projections.

---

## ✨ What is Runway?

Most finance apps show you where your money *went*.

Runway shows you whether you can *afford what’s coming next*.

It’s a sandbox-style planning tool designed to answer questions like:
- “Can we afford this holiday?”
- “What happens if groceries run high this month?”
- “How long does my money last if income changes?”
- “Are we still safe if I spend this now?”

All without linking bank accounts or sharing sensitive data.

---

## 🧠 Core Ideas

- **Forecast-first**  
  The app opens directly to a month-by-month projection of your balance.

- **Manual by design**  
  You stay in full control. Nothing is imported, synced, or assumed.

- **Scenario-aware**  
  Model best-case, expected, and worst-case spending.

- **People & Shared costs**  
  Support for individuals and shared household expenses with split rules.

- **Green / Yellow / Red safety indicators**  
  Instantly see when projections fall below your minimum savings threshold.

---

## 🧩 How it works

Runway is built around composable “segments”:
- Income
- Subscriptions
- Fixed expenses
- Variable spending (ranges)
- One-off events (holidays, big purchases, etc.)

Each segment contributes to a unified cashflow forecast.
All segments are visible at once — no hidden dashboards, no mode switching.

---

## 🔒 Privacy-first by default

- No bank connections
- No external APIs
- No background syncing
- Data stays local

Runway treats your finances as a **planning problem**, not a data-mining opportunity.
